Refer With Confidence

Experienced Personal Injury Counsel for Your Referred Clients

When a case needs focused personal injury resources, our team can review the facts quickly and discuss whether Frank Penney Injury Law is the right fit.

We handle serious injury matters across California, including vehicle collisions, premises liability, dog bites, wrongful death, and other plaintiff-side personal injury claims.

We respect attorney referral relationships, communication preferences, and client trust. Referral arrangements are handled in compliance with applicable professional rules.
