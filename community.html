Community & Charitable Giving

Supporting the Neighborhoods We Practice In

Frank Penney Injury Law supports youth sports, local schools, cancer support programs, and legal aid organizations across Sacramento, Placer County, and Northern California. Our most recent gift was $12,000 to the Sacramento Children's Home in July 2025. These are the same communities our clients and our staff live in.

Sacramento Children's Home

A $12,000 Gift to Sacramento's Children

In July 2025, our firm presented the Sacramento Children's Home with a check for $12,000 — memo line: Bank on Frank. The organization serves children and families throughout the Sacramento region, and it is the kind of work that never makes headlines but changes outcomes for kids every single day.

This one mattered enough that we closed the office and brought the whole team.

Why It Matters To Us

Rooted in Northern California

Personal injury work puts you in the middle of the worst weeks of people's lives. You meet families whose income stopped, whose kids' routines fell apart, whose parents are suddenly caregivers. Over thirty years, that has made one thing plain: the safety net around a family matters as much as the settlement.

So we give where we practice — the schools our staff's kids attend, the teams they coach, the crisis programs our clients get referred to. Learn more about our firm or read Frank Penney's biography.

Common Questions

Community Involvement FAQs

Which organizations does Frank Penney Injury Law support?

Our firm has supported youth sports programs, local schools, cancer support services, legal aid organizations, and community nonprofits across Sacramento and Placer County. Organizations we have supported include the Sacramento Children's Home, Wellness Within, Friends of Camp Sacramento, and the California Rural Legal Assistance Foundation.

How much did the firm donate to the Sacramento Children's Home?

Frank Penney Injury Law presented the Sacramento Children's Home with a $12,000 donation in July 2025 to support its work with children and families in the Sacramento region. Our whole team came out for the presentation.

Does the firm sponsor youth sports teams?

Yes. The firm has sponsored Twelve Bridges High School football and basketball, the Twelve Bridges Elementary School fun run, John Adams Academy fundraisers, Sierra College softball, and the Sacramento Wrestling Federation.

How does the firm choose which organizations to support?

We focus on organizations rooted in the Northern California communities we serve, with an emphasis on programs for children and families, youth athletics, education, and access to legal help. Most of our giving goes to groups our attorneys and staff have a personal connection to.
