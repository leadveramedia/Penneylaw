We've Received Your Message

Thank you for trusting us with this. A lawyer will call you privately — you will not be asked to write anything down, and nothing has been filed anywhere.

What Happens Next

  • 1

    Someone from our office will call you

    Usually within one business day. The call will come from (888) 888-0566 or a local Northern California number. We will say only that we are returning your message, and nothing more, in case someone else answers.

  • 2

    You decide how much to tell us, and when

    We need enough to know whether we can help. You can stop at any point, ask to continue another day, or ask that a specific person not be contacted. What you tell us is confidential whether or not you go on to hire anyone.

  • 3

    Nothing moves without your written agreement

    If the matter fits this litigation, we work with co-counsel who handle these claims nationally, and we refer matters outside California to counsel licensed in your state. We will explain that arrangement and any fee sharing in writing, and get your agreement, before your information goes to anyone else.

If you would rather we called differently

Call us at (888) 888-0566 and say you submitted a form and would like to set a time, ask for a blocked or withheld number, or give us a different phone number to use. Ask for whatever makes this easier — it is a normal request and we handle it often.

Support available right now

None of these organizations are connected to this firm, none of them will ask you about a lawsuit, and all of them are free.

  • RAINN National Sexual Assault Hotline — 800-656-4673, 24 hours a day, confidential.
  • Childhelp National Child Abuse Hotline — 800-422-4453, 24 hours a day.
  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline — call or text 988.
  • NCMEC CyberTipline — to report online child exploitation, report.cybertip.org.

If a child is in immediate danger, call 911 first. A legal claim can wait.
